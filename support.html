Common questions

The scan didn't find my record. Photograph the front cover in even light so it fills the frame, or tap “Type it” and search by artist and album.

Why do pressings have different prices? Original pressings, reissues and regional editions sell for very different amounts. Pick the one that matches the label and catalog number on your copy.

How do I restore my subscription? Settings → Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.

How do I cancel? iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Needla.
