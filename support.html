Support

Questions, answered

01

Write to us

netkaktakto@gmail.com reaches a person; replies usually take under two working days.

02

The scan did not find my record

Photograph the front of the sleeve in even light and fill the frame, or tap Type it and search by artist and album.

03

Why does one album show many prices?

Each pressing sells for its own price. Match the country, label and catalog number on your copy, then tap that pressing.

04

How does grading work?

Answer four checks (surface, sound, sleeve, seams). Needla maps them to a Mint-to-Good grade and shows your copy’s price at that grade.

05

Restore or cancel

Restore: Settings → Restore purchases with the same Apple ID. Cancel: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Needla.

06

Delete everything

Needla keeps the crate only on your iPhone, so deleting the app removes it. Nothing is held on a Needla account because there is none.
