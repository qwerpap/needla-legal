Needla — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Needla helps you identify vinyl records, find their pressing and estimate their value. We built it to collect as little as possible.

Your crate

The records you save, their condition grades and your answers in onboarding are stored only on your device. We never receive them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Photos you scan

When you scan a sleeve (and only after you agree on the in-app consent screen), the photo is sent over an encrypted connection to our server, which passes it to OpenAI to read the artist, title and catalog number. The photo is used only to answer that request and is not stored by us. OpenAI processes it under its API data policy, which does not use API data to train models.

Record lookups

Searches, pressings and prices are requested from the public Discogs database. These requests contain the search text or record ID only and are not linked to you.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details. To check whether your subscription is active and to show the right offer, the app uses Adapty, which receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase history and basic in-app events (for example, that a paywall was shown). This data is not linked to your identity and is not used for tracking or advertising.

What we don't do

No accounts, no advertising SDKs, no tracking across apps or websites, no sale or sharing of personal data.

Service providers

Google Cloud / Firebase (hosts our server), OpenAI (sleeve reading), Adapty (subscription status), Discogs (public record database).

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal data.
