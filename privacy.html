Privacy

Last updated October 6, 2026

01

Short version

Your crate stays on your iPhone. A sleeve photo leaves it once, to be read, and only after you agree. Nothing else about you is collected by us.

02

Your crate

The records you save, the grades you give them, your onboarding answers and the app’s settings are kept in Needla’s own storage on the device. We never receive them. Deleting the app deletes them.

03

Sleeve photos

If you scan a sleeve, and only after you tap agree on the consent screen, the photo goes over an encrypted connection to our server function on Google Cloud (Firebase). That function hands it to a vision model (an OpenAI model that Replicate runs) with one job: read the artist, the title and the catalog number. We use the answer to look the record up and do not keep the photo. Replicate and OpenAI handle API requests under their own policies. Say no to the consent screen and you can still find records by typing.

04

Record look-ups

Pressings, prices and want/have counts are asked from the public Discogs database straight from your iPhone. A request contains the text you searched or a release number, not your name, and Discogs sees your IP address as any website would. Cover thumbnails are loaded from Discogs the same way.

05

Camera

The camera is only used for the sleeve scan. You can switch it off in iOS Settings and keep using search by name.

06

Purchases

Apple processes payment and we never see card details. To show the right plan and unlock Pro, the app uses Adapty, which gets an anonymous install ID, purchase status and basic in-app events such as “paywall shown”. It is not linked to who you are.

07

What is not here

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising code and no tracking across apps or sites.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data.
08

Who handles data for us

Google Cloud / Firebase (server function), Replicate and OpenAI (sleeve reading), Discogs (record database), Adapty (subscription status), Apple (payments). Needla is independent and is not endorsed by any of them.

09

Children and changes

Needla is not made for children under 13. If this page changes, the date at the top changes too. Questions: netkaktakto@gmail.com.
