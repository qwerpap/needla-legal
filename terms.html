Subscriptions

Needla Pro is an auto-renewing subscription (weekly or yearly). Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation of purchase. It renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period; your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the period ends. A free trial, if offered, converts to a paid subscription unless cancelled before it ends. Manage or cancel in your Apple ID settings.

Values are estimates based on current Discogs marketplace listings and your own condition answers. They are not professional appraisals, offers to buy, or insurance valuations. Record data comes from Discogs; Needla is not affiliated with Discogs.
